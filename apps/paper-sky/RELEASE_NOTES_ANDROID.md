Paper Sky for Android: the drifting paper-plane sky as a live wallpaper for your home screen and lock screen.

**Install**
1. Download `PaperSky-Android-*.apk` on your phone and open it.
2. Android asks to allow installs from your browser/files app: allow it for this install (Settings › Apps › Special access › Install unknown apps).
3. Open **Paper Sky**, play with it, tap **⋯ › Set as wallpaper**, choose **Home and lock screens**.

**How it behaves**
- Home screen: planes follow your finger; swiping between home pages adds a gentle parallax.
- Lock screen: the sky animates on its own (Android keeps lock-screen touches for itself). Your PIN/pattern/fingerprint is Android's; Paper Sky never sees it.
- 30 fps, 20 fps in Low power or Battery Saver; stops drawing whenever it isn't visible.

Signed with a development key (not from the Play Store), Android 8.0+.
