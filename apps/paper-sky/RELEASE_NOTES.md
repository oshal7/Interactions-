Paper Sky for macOS: the drifting paper-plane sky as a screen saver, a live wallpaper and an interactive ambient mode.

**Downloads**
- `PaperSky-ScreenSaver-*.zip`: unzip, double-click **Paper Sky.saver**, choose *Install*.
- `PaperSky-App-*.zip`: unzip, move **Paper Sky.app** to Applications, open it; a ✈︎ appears in the menu bar.

These builds are ad-hoc signed, not notarized. The first time, macOS will say it can't verify the developer:
open **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** (or right-click the file › Open).

Your password is never handled by Paper Sky. Locking uses the real macOS screen saver lock.
Setup and resource usage: see the Paper Sky page in the Interaction Lab site.
