Make your lock screen

iPhones don't allow animated or interactive lock screens from apps. You can still have the sky there: save a still at your phone's exact resolution, or record a short loop and turn it into a Live Photo wallpaper.

Setting it on your phone.

iPhone, still wallpaper

  1. Open this page on the iPhone, pick your model, tap Save still, then Save Image.
  2. Photos › the image › Share › Use as Wallpaper, or press and hold the lock screen › + › Photos.

iPhone, moving wallpaper (Live Photo)

  1. Tap Record 6 s loop and save the video.
  2. Convert it to a Live Photo with a video-to-Live-Photo app (for example intoLive), then set that Live Photo as the lock screen wallpaper.
  3. iOS plays a Live Photo wallpaper briefly when you wake the phone. It can't be touched or run continuously; that's an iOS limit.

Want it interactive?

  1. iPhone: open the Paper Sky app page in Safari › Share › Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen, follows your finger, plays the ambient sound, and works offline.
  2. Android: install the Paper Sky live wallpaper app from the Paper Sky page. It moves on the home and lock screens and follows your finger on the home screen.
